# Hardware Wars

An 80s-style arcade shooter based on the 1978 short film *Hardware Wars*. Fly Fluke Starbucker past steam irons and toasters to rescue Princess Anne-Droid from Darph Nader.

This is a fan-made tribute. The music is original chiptune written for the game.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. It works on phones.

- Drag anywhere to fly. Your ship fires on its own.
- Tap **FARCE** to clear enemy fire.
- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to move, Space for the Farce, P to pause.
